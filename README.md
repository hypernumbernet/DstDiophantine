# DstDiophantine

Lean 4 development of **Double Spacetime Theory** (DST), together with an
amplification programme for classical Diophantine conjectures.

The machine-checked core is an algebraic bound, a motor calculus, and a
shared amplification no-go. Unconditional Fermat, Beal, abc, the Riemann
Hypothesis, Goldbach, Polignac, and Collatz remain open. Open work is named
as a residual or a `*Bridge` proposition. Eight classical theorems that
mathlib does not supply at this pin are explicit `axiom`s. There is no
`sorry`.

Where a paper draft and the Lean development disagree, the Lean development
is the record. A paper title is the title of that source.

Entry point for the Diophantine path:
[`DstDiophantine/Basic.lean`](DstDiophantine/Basic.lean)
(`import DstDiophantine.Basic`). Gravity, D4L, and the conformal probe are
parallel tracks. Their barrels are not re-exported from `Basic`.

## Double Spacetime Theory

DST refuses a single shared spacetime continuum. Each massive particle
carries its own pair of compact Minkowski structures inside the
16-real-dimensional biquaternion algebra
$\mathbb{H} \otimes_{\mathbb{R}} \mathbb{H} \cong \mathrm{Cl}(3,1)$:

- **usual** spacetime, basis $(j, kI, kJ, kK)$
- **dual** spacetime, basis $(k, jI, jJ, jK)$

The dual map $X \mapsto Xi$ reverses the arrow of time and preserves the
Minkowski norm. Lorentz transformations are generated by rotors on each
sector. Gravity and inertia are read as the **torsional mismatch**

$$
\Omega = R_{\mathrm{usual}}^{\dagger}\, R_{\mathrm{dual}},
\qquad
J = \tfrac12\sum_{a=1}^{3}(\alpha_a^2 - \beta_a^2).
$$

$J$ is a dimensionless scalar on rapidity space. The mass partner is
$M = \tfrac12\sum_a(\alpha_a^2 + \beta_a^2)$. The normalised height is
$J_{\mathrm{norm}} = J / (3\pi^2/8)$, so the admissible ceiling
$|J_{\mathrm{norm}}| \le 1$ is the same statement as $|J| \le 3\pi^2/8$.

Vanishing $J$ does not force $\Omega = 1$. There is a balanced massive seed
with $J = 0$, $M > 0$, and $\Omega \ne 1$. The identity $\Omega = 1$ holds
exactly when the two rotors coincide.

Translation is supplied by projective geometric algebra $G(3,1,1)$: one null
vector $e_4$, ten bivector generators, and motors $M = RT$. The ten
bivectors split as three boosts, three rotations, and four null
translations, and under the commutator they are the Poincaré algebra.
The ordered product $RT$ is the motor. It equals the Banach exponential of
the combined bivector when torsion and translation commute, and there are
motors for which the two disagree. The ordered product and the third-order
Baker–Campbell–Hausdorff exponential share a 3-jet.

## Algebraic core

Proved in [`DstDiophantine/Algebra.lean`](DstDiophantine/Algebra.lean) and
the discrete companion
[`papers/discrete-dual-spacetime.tex`](papers/discrete-dual-spacetime.tex).

- Clifford generators, the Poincaré span, and duality as a complex structure
  on the Lorentz sector: every boost is the dual of a rotation, and duality
  reverses $J$ while preserving $M$.
- Null translators. The exponential of a null generator truncates,
  $\exp(\Omega_{\mathrm{trans}}) = 1 + \Omega_{\mathrm{trans}}$, and
  translators multiply by adding coefficients.
- Sandwich conjugation. A torsion rotor sends translators to translators
  and torsion rotors to torsion rotors. A pure boost conjugates a translator
  by a diagonal scaling of the null coefficients.
- The admissible cone: nonnegative rapidities with
  $\alpha_a + \beta_a \le \pi/2$ on each axis, and
  $|J_{\mathrm{norm}}| \le 1$ on that cone. The bound is sharp.
- Cyclic exponentials are $2\pi$-periodic. Hyperbolic exponentials are not.
  Placing boosts on a $2\pi$-torus is a modelling choice. Admissibility is a
  further hypothesis on that torus, not a consequence of discretising it.
- The image of $(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^6$ under the torsion-rotor map is
  finite. Same-axis generators commute, so a one-axis exponential
  factorises. The bracket of `hyperbolic 0` with `cyclic 1` is nonzero, so
  that factorisation stays on one axis, and the discrete rotor image is not
  a Cartesian product of three one-axis images.

The shared amplification no-go lives in
[`Framework/Amplification.lean`](DstDiophantine/Framework/Amplification.lean).
A seed that amplifies past the admissible ceiling cannot exist. The
argument does not depend on which Diophantine equation produced the seed.

## Diophantine programme

Integers embed into PGA motors by rotors and null translators. A power-sum
relation such as $a^n + b^n = c^n$ or $A^x + B^y = C^z$ becomes
`powerSumMotor = 1`. Dual-spacetime rapidities are discretised to a finite
torus $(\mathbb{Z}/N\mathbb{Z})^6$, so each problem is a search over an
admissible lattice.

Developed in [`DstDiophantine/Framework/`](DstDiophantine/Framework/) and
[`papers/dst-diophantine.tex`](papers/dst-diophantine.tex).

| Layer | Content | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Faithful encoding | Additive relations as null-translator motors | Proved |
| 2. Amplification no-go | No seed amplifies past the admissible ceiling | Proved |
| 3. Bridge or residual | A classical solution would produce such a witness, or a named leaf after a closed case split | Open |

The proof challenge is layer 3: close a live residual, prove a live
`*Bridge`, or replace an `axiom` with an internal proof. Diagnostic bridges
are recorded so they can be told apart from the live route.
`FermatAdmissibleBridge`, `FermatModularBridge`, `BealAdmissibleBridge`,
`BealModularBridge`, and `BealWindingBridge` are diagnostic.
`BealModularBridge` is incompatible independently of the equation.
`AbcAdmissibleBridge` is false: the triple $(1,8,9)$ has quality greater
than $1$ and an admissible mismatch.
`BealCGARealization` is bookkeeping. Under coprimality it forces a unit
base. It is not a geometric proof of Beal.

### Chart and the direction of gravity

On every static radial-boost chart the teleparallel density is
$T = (4/r^2)(\cosh\sqrt{2J} - 1)$. Restricted to the admissible cone,
$r^2 T$ lies in the open interval $(-8, 27)$, and both ends of the exact
window $4(\cos\varphi_{\max}-1) \le r^2 T \le 4(\cosh\varphi_{\max}-1)$ are
attained. A real radial boost cannot produce $T < 0$; a negative
$T$ on the cone uses the elliptic sector. Exterior Schwarzschild is the
vacuum gauge $A = 1 - r_s/r$. The identification $J_{\mathrm{field}} = \tfrac12 T$
holds on at most one sphere.

The same value of $J$ does not determine $T$ for every tetrad. An inertial-plane
boost of any rapidity profile induces the Minkowski metric and kills the
teleparallel quadratic, while the exterior Schwarzschild coframe with that
same $J$ has positive $T$.

The static Killing field is the Poincaré bivector
$K = \tfrac{\kappa}{2} B^{+} + \tfrac{\nu}{2} N_0$: a pure boost about an
axis displaced inward by $\ell = \nu/\kappa$. On the exterior chart
$\ell \cdot \partial_r\sqrt{A} = 1$, so the inward acceleration of a hovering
observer is $1/\ell$. The axis reaches the observer at the horizon and
recedes to infinity far away. For any Poincaré bivector the squared Killing
norm has d'Alembertian $8J$.

A body released from rest in the flat flow of $K$ accelerates toward the
axis at $1/\ell$ and meets the light cone of the axis in proper time $\ell$.
`RadialFall` keeps the same law on the exterior, with chart distance
$\ell(r)$ in place of the flat interval. The powers of the speed cancel and
$d^2 r/d\tau^2 = -r_s/(2r^2)$. One integration recovers
$(dr/d\tau)^2 = E^2 - A$.

### Scales that stay inputs

`SI` and `NewtonFromLight` are SI stand-ins. Comparing $c$ with $G$ does not
derive $G$. `EventBoundary` reads the admissible ceiling as a finite redshift
floor; a one-way horizon $A = 0$ does not form on that chart.
`CoulombFromDual` and `ElectronShell` are Coulombic stand-ins. They do not
derive $\alpha$ or $\lambda$.

`CompactS3` gives $v^2 = (GM/R)\, f(r/R)$. The cotangent potential is
radially harmonic, with Newtonian germ $-GM/r + GMr/(3R^2)$. The profile $f$
has a unique minimum on $(0, \pi/2)$ with $1.35 < f_0 < 1.41$, and
$f < (11/10) f_0$ on $[1, 6/5]$. The acceleration stays center-directed on
$(0, \pi R)$. The Milky Way window used there is $8\,\mathrm{kpc} < R < 9\,\mathrm{kpc}$.

`NuclearLayer` compares saturation density, binding energy per nucleon, and
the pion length. Under $\ell = \lambda_\pi$ every equal-scale node lies
below $1\,\mathrm{fm}$. The estimate $A \sim 300$ is an external heuristic.
`ParticleStability` records, on the same stand-ins, $1836 < m_p/m_e < 1837$,
and that a hundred nuclear bindings and twenty optical wells each lie below
the proton rest energy.

`BaryonAsymmetry` computes the two signs of a vacuum rapidity. Both $J$ and
$M$ are even in that rapidity, and $J/M = \pm 1$ when both are nonzero. On a
one-axis balance the two signs differ in mass by a term linear in the shift.
Equal nonzero rapidities give $J = 0$, $M > 0$, and a relative rotor
different from $1$. No numerical baryon yield is derived.

### Particle action

[`DualRotorDynamics.lean`](DstDiophantine/Gravity/DualRotorDynamics.lean)
is the Euler–Lagrange system of the written opposite-sign density. In the
channels $\sigma = \phi + \theta$ and $\delta = \phi - \theta$ the mismatch is
free, $\ddot\delta = 0$, and the common rapidity is sourced,
$\ddot\sigma = -2m\delta$. An affine lag integrates to a cubic $\sigma$.
The Jacobi integral reduces to initial data and is indefinite.

The neighbouring same-sign kinetic model is the oscillator
$\ddot\delta + 2m\delta = 0$ at frequency $\sqrt{2m}$, with a free common
rapidity. That frequency meets the Compton value $m$ only at $m = 0$ and
$m = 2$. A runaway $\ddot\delta = 2m\delta$ is a third sign pattern. It is
not the written action.

Stiffness $V = (m/2)\sum_a(\alpha_a - \beta_a)^2$, unsigned mass $M$, and
free fall $\Omega = 1$ are three different quantities. On one axis
$V = 2m(M - \sigma^2/4)$. A cross-axis shield can have $J = 0$, $M > 0$, and
$V > 0$. The oscillator potential is not the parameter-space Killing
quadratic. Identifying the free lag with a de Broglie phase is a reading of
this action. The note is
[`papers/dst-dynamic-equation.tex`](papers/dst-dynamic-equation.tex).

On one axis the factorised rotor splits the null ideal: the usual rapidity
scales one null plane and the dual angle rotates the complementary plane.
Off axis a usual generator and a dual generator anticommute, and the sign of
$p^2 - q^2$ selects one hyperbolic, elliptic, or parabolic rotor. The
parabolic rotor is a null rotation. Its truncations form a one-parameter
group of Minkowski isometries, fixing one null line, and the same
coefficients act on translations.

### Signed electrostatic force

On the equal-scale locus $\alpha = \beta = \ell/r$ the electromagnetic force
is the Coulomb force divided by the interference factor
$\gamma_s(x) = \cosh x\cos x - \sinh x\sin x$, with phase $x = \ell/(2r)$.
The sign of $\gamma_s$ is the repulsion. The length $\ell$ is an input.
Bohr's $n^2$ and the fine-structure constant are not derived. No centrifugal
term is added.

Zeros of $\gamma_s$ are simple, one in each interval $[n\pi,(n+1)\pi]$, and
the first lies in $(\pi/4, \pi/2)$. The outer well is the region outside that
first node, where an electron and a proton attract. The first repulsive
shell lies between the first and second nodes, and the signs reverse there.
Attraction, counted positive, strengthens throughout the outer well, so a
one-center pair has no stationary radius. The node is a pole of the force.

A circular path in the outer well is radially restoring above one radius and
unstable between that radius and the node. The restoring threshold is the
unique phase in $(1/2, 3/5)$ where $d\ln Y/d\ln x = 3$ for
$Y = 4x^2/\gamma_s$, so the radius lies in $(5\ell/6, \ell)$. The energy of
those circular paths is negative and strictly least at the same radius.
The electrostatic force does not vanish there. The path is not a shell and
not $a_0$.

The first repulsive shell has one extremum of $Y$, at a phase in
$(5/2, \pi)$, so the radius lies in $(\ell/(2\pi), \ell/5)$. Counted with
attraction positive, the value there lies in $(-3, 0)$. At phase $\pi$ the
outward push is stronger. The figure marks that stationary point of the
repulsive force. The horizontal axis is distance in units of $\ell$.

The counts $2$, $8$, $18$, and $32$ are two chiral seats on the modes
through degrees $0$ through $3$ (monopole $1$, dipole $3$, traceless
quadrupole $5$, traceless octupole $7$). Degree by degree the same seats
number $2$, $6$, $10$, and $14$. A seat is a pattern on directions. The
monopole is independent of direction. A dipole changes sign across a plane.
A nonzero traceless quadrupole is even under reversal, takes both signs, and
has a nodal direction. A nonzero traceless octupole changes sign under
reversal. These counts are not indices of the nodes of $\gamma_s$, and they
are not spectroscopic labels.

Two electrons in the outer well are not simultaneously force-free, in any
placement, once the nuclear charge is at least $1/4$. A diameter, for every
$Z \ge 1$, and a cube of eight electrons have exactly one radial balance in
the first repulsive shell. The diameter is an angular maximum. Among the
regular polyhedra, only the dodecahedron has a chord shorter than its
radius. A tetrahedron, an octahedron, a cube, and an icosahedron have no
outer radial root once $Z \ge 1$, $2$, $3$, and $5$. For every $Z \ge 8$ the
dodecahedron has exactly one radial root before that chord meets the first
node, at a radius strictly between $7\ell/10$ and $10\ell/7$, and the
outward force restores it. Twenty is a vertex count.

On the symmetric midplane pair, with both separations in the outer well,
the two force components do not vanish together at any ratio. The square of
that family closes in the reversed shell. The water molecule places the
nucleus at the origin and one proton on each of two rays, with one bonding
electron on each ray, closer to the nucleus than its proton. While those
electrons repel, the other bond drives each bonding electron toward a wider
angle. An electron on the same ray adds nothing. One that repels from the
side toward the other bond widens the angle, and one that repels from the
opposite side narrows it. On the straight line the two bonds exert no
tangential force, and an electron on the opposite axis takes the arrangement
off the line. With each electron between the nucleus and its proton, the
outward sum is strictly negative once $Z \ge 1/4$.

On a segment longer than twice the first-node radius, an electron that stays
in the outer well of both nuclei is drawn from the midpoint toward the
larger charge. The unique cancellation lies closer to the smaller charge.
That direction is the electronegativity of the larger charge. No laboratory
scale is read off.

The side of the reversed-shell square is not the Bohr radius. The laboratory
sign at the Bohr radius is attraction, so that radius lies in the outer well.
The note is
[`papers/dst-electron-shell.tex`](papers/dst-electron-shell.tex).

### Blackbody occupation

[`Blackbody.lean`](DstDiophantine/Gravity/Blackbody.lean), narrated in the
heat section of the same note. On a harmonic ladder of spacing $x > 0$, in
units of $k_B T$, the mean number of quanta above the ground state is
$1/(e^x - 1)$. The mean energy above the ground state is strictly less than
$k_B T$. For $n \ge 2$ the shape $x^n/(e^x - 1)$ has one positive maximum,
in $(n-1, n)$: the frequency density peaks in $(5/2, 3)$ and the wavelength
density in $(9/2, 5)$. The maximizing wavelength times the temperature is
constant. The equipartition density $T\nu^2$ has no maximum. The integral of
$x^3/(e^x - 1)$ equals $\pi^4/15$, so an enclosure whose modes grow as
$\nu^2$ scales as $T^4$. The constants $h$, $k_B$, $c$, and that mode growth
are inputs. They are not roots of $\gamma_s$.

### Faraday six-space and dual-only control

Faraday coefficients occupy the same six generators as the torsional
bivector: electric on the boosts, magnetic on the rotations. Duality sends
$(E, B)$ to $(B, -E)$ and has period $4$. The quadratic is
$J = \tfrac12(E^2 - B^2)$. The first-order sandwich increment on $e_0 + v$ is
the Lorentz 4-force of the laboratory time-reversed pair $(E, -B)$. A
circular wave of helicity $\pm 1$ is null at every phase, with Poynting
$\sigma E_0^2$.

A spatial axis splits the six-space into an $\mathfrak{so}(2,1)$ stabilizer
and a complement that moves the axis. Compact $\mathrm{SU}(2)$ in this
development is the dual rotor, with $IJ = K$. It is not a symmetry of one
fixed projector.

Dual-only motion conserves $J + M$ and cannot raise $J$. It reaches a shield

$$
M_{\mathrm{norm}} \le \tfrac56 + \tfrac32 J_{\mathrm{norm}}^2,

effective factor $\gamma_{\mathrm{eff}}$ returns to $1$ at a dual angle
strictly before the coaxial shield.

`EMControl` reads a dual-only increment as magnetic superposition and as the

### Lensing and the return of a ray

units of $GM/c^3$. The shortest interior visit exceeds $20$ on the one-axis
saturation and $280$ on the three-axis ceiling.

## Four-valued logic

Parallel track, barrel [`DstDiophantine/Logic.lean`](DstDiophantine/Logic.lean).
The note is [`papers/dst-4-valued-logic.tex`](papers/dst-4-valued-logic.tex).

A proposition is an admissible torsion configuration. The observable is
$J_{\mathrm{norm}} \in [-1,1]$, and collapse yields four labels. The walls
$\pm 1$ are the two-valued fragment: classical truth is the positive wall.
Balance is an information bottom, not a fifth label. Conjunction with
negation never saturates the negative wall, so $\{P, \neg P\}$ does not
explode. The two designated predicates, theoremhood and non-refutation, have
no common residual on the height. The Gödel residuum of $\min$ and the
regime implication disagree at $U \to T$ and at $B \to T$.

The amplitude layer carries a second observable, the mass. Vacuum and
balanced massive configurations can share the label $T$. Scaling can send a
balanced seed out of the cone while the signed-height classifier still
reads $T$. Height and winding cannot be admissible together. The Killing
form is indefinite, so self-overlap is not a Born probability.

Beal and Fermat status boards live in `Logic/Example/`. Closed slices,
diagnostic bridges, and live residuals receive different labels. The boards
do not entail the classical conjectures.

### Dual Hilbert layer

`Logic/Quantum/` is the same logic, not a separate physics track. The notes
are [`papers/dst-de-broglie-wave.tex`](papers/dst-de-broglie-wave.tex) and
[`papers/dst-quantum-gravity.tex`](papers/dst-quantum-gravity.tex).

The dual rotor is $\mathrm{SU}(2)$. Its trace is $2\cos(\|\beta\|/2)$, the
sum of the two opposite computational amplitudes. The spin-up matrix element
along the observer axis is $e^{-i\theta/2}$. The inverse rotor is the
opposite rapidity. Rotors along a common ray compose by adding the scale,
and the relative rotor on that ray is a single difference. The character of
a product records the alignment of the two axes. Perpendicular $\pi$
rotations have group commutator $-I$, and a $2\pi$ rotation about the
observer axis is $-I$.

The usual-sector rotor is the hyperbolic Rodrigues formula. It is Hermitian
of determinant $1$. A nonzero usual boost is not unitary.

The momentum-space Dirac operator is the Clifford factor of the Minkowski
quadratic $Q(p)$. On the mass shell $Q(p) = -m^2$ with $m \ne 0$, a dual
spinor $u$ determines a Dirac solution whose rest-frame value is $(u, iu)$.
Dual rotation of $u$ stays on that shell. A longitudinal boost of rest
momentum is on-shell, and it equals the on-shell Weyl construction after
the chiral factor $V(\alpha) = \cosh(\alpha/2)\, I + \sinh(\alpha/2)\, \sigma_z$.
Dual rotation about the boost axis commutes with $V(\alpha)$. A perpendicular
dual rotation need not. Rest-frame overlap of dual-rotated solutions is
twice the dual-rotor amplitude. That overlap identity is a rest-frame
statement.

The commuting left ideal in the PGA has real dimension $8$ and splits as a
four-dimensional Minkowski core plus a null copy, so it is reducible as a
left PGA-module. Dirac spinors have the same real dimension. $\mathrm{Cl}(3,1)$
and $\mathrm{Cl}(9,1)$ have real dimensions $16$ and $1024$ and are not
isomorphic. The four D4L labels are not four orthogonal rays in $\mathbb{C}^2$.
The orthomodular (Sasaki) implication detaches for every pair. The classical
material expansion detaches exactly on compatible pairs.

The dual-rotor action on the Weyl parameter is internal. This layer does not
produce a spacetime Dirac equation, a mass from torsion, or covariance under
the full rotor group. The dual-rotor character, the spinor matrix element,
and a scalar $\mathrm{U}(1)$ phase are three distinct invariants. A finite
discrete rotor image is not a count of generations. The light-cone split
$8+8$ is not the six torsional generators, and the super-Poincaré count used
in the string comparison is not the ten PGA generators.

## Conformal probe

[`DstDiophantine/CGA.lean`](DstDiophantine/CGA.lean) is a one-dimensional
$\mathrm{Cl}(2,1)$ probe of the dilation sector that $G(3,1,1)$ does not
supply. Conformal points are null. Integers land on that null cone.
The point $2^{4/3}$ does not, although it does lie on the $3$-power lattice.
Integer dilations act on the integer null lattice, and the Pythagorean ratio
$5/3$ is not one of them. PGA integer-rotor height is not globally bounded.

The probe is diagnostic. It is the source of the Beal bookkeeping predicates
`IsCGAIntegerPoint`, `IsCGAPowerLatticePoint`, and `IsCGAIntegerDilation`.
It is not a three-dimensional conformal model, and it is not a proof of Beal.

## Build

The toolchain is pinned in [`lean-toolchain`](lean-toolchain) at
`leanprover/lean4:v4.34.0-rc1`. mathlib is the matching revision in
[`lakefile.toml`](lakefile.toml). Install [elan](https://github.com/leanprover/elan),
then from the repository root:

```bash
lake build
```

A module can be built on its own. The gravity and logic barrels do not pull
each other in through `Basic`:

```bash
lake build DstDiophantine.Gravity
lake build DstDiophantine.Logic
lake build DstDiophantine.Logic.Quantum.DiracEquation
lake build DstDiophantine.CGA
```

Continuous integration
([`.github/workflows/lean_action_ci.yml`](.github/workflows/lean_action_ci.yml))
runs that build with
[lean-action](https://github.com/leanprover/lean-action) and generates
documentation with
[docgen-action](https://github.com/leanprover-community/docgen-action) on
every push and pull request. A toolchain change on `main` or `master` is
tagged by
[`.github/workflows/create-release.yml`](.github/workflows/create-release.yml).
Dependency updates are manual, through
[`.github/workflows/update.yml`](.github/workflows/update.yml).

`FoundationRegression` is the CI-facing sample of the Diophantine path,
including the Beal and Fermat atlases. A green build of `Logic` can still
replay existing `flexible-simp` and `unusedSimpArgs` warnings in `Spinor`,
`DualRodrigues`, `UsualRodrigues`, and older `DiracEquation` simp lists.
